I've released a new guide! It's called How to get X and Y coordinates quickly without changing programs. It's made to help balloon and shell developers who use a drawing program that doesn't display coordinates, and makes it easy to line up images precisely without using an additional program.

This is a quick and fairly casual guide, so I decided to just write it on the Ukagaka Dream Team Forum. I think it would also benefit from others chiming in and adding specific notes for the art programs they use, so the forum seems a good venue!

I hope it helps someone out there. This is definitely my favorite technique I've come up with so far for measuring coordinates, and I've tried several!
