I participated in a game of Telephone hosted by Kipali, which produced the ghost Let's play Telephone!

I'd like to say I'm posting this in a timely manner, but I'm multiple months late. The ghost was posted in August, and unfortunately I have just had a lot going on IRL that has kept me from posting about it. But, better late than never! The ghost is now listed on my ghost index, and I thought it might be fun to write up a little retrospective.

This ghost was a lot of fun to work on! I found it very difficult to come up with a topic to start my own chain with, but after that it was easier to respond to each section I was given. Unfortunately, I was very unwell while this game was going on, so it was a big struggle for me to keep up and I didn't get to write as much as I wanted... but maybe that's for the best, since I ended up writing some enormous word groups as it is anyway.

I also spent a good chunk of time also analyzing the text I was given, looking at the placement of tags, use of punctuation, and more, to try and determine who I was following. In the end, I got it right! Playing detective that way was really fun. It's the sort of thing I always hope to be able to do in Ghost Masquerade, but usually do not get to do because there are so few English ghosts that I can figure things out mostly by process of elimination.

At first I thought it might be fun to mimic the style of how each segment I was given was written. This is something I'm pretty good at, since when I write ghosts with Galla, I make an effort to ensure the formatting and style of our dialogues is similar and you can't tell who wrote what just by reading it. However, it didn't seem like anyone else was doing the same, so I dropped it after 1 round and started formatting dialogues in the way most comfortable to me. I do think it would have been interesting to see each chain have a different formatting style, but I think most people probably aren't focusing on those details rather than the writing!

Reading the final chains was so much fun. Some of them change immediately, while others have a very slow transition and mostly survive intact. Some of them stick the landing, while others... yeah, I did not stick the landing on mine. I didn't have quite enough context to understand what was going on, so I just made something up, and was nowhere near what had come before me. Whoops!

I hope it's something we can do again sometime, and that next time I'll be feeling better and can give it my all!
