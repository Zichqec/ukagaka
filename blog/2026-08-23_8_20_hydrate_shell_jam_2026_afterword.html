The results of 8/20 Hydrate Shell Jam 2026 are here! Please check out the results page to see each individual entry and where you can download them.

This year's turnout is the smallest that this event has ever had. It seems that a lot of folks were busy this year! But, honestly, that's probably for the best. I've been unwell lately (as evidenced by the fact I was only able to scrape together one barebones entry for the event) so having a small amount of entries to deal with has made pulling the results together much more manageable.

I love that, despite the small number of shells, there is still a selection of normal drinks vs weird things that only technically count. They're pretty evenly split, too. I have to wonder if that will always be true for as long as the event runs, or if one year we'll see it swing fully in one direction!

Thanks again to everyone who participated, and to those folks who are still working on new shells or shell updates outside the event. I look forward to doing it again next year!
