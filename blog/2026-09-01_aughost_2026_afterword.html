Aughost 2026 is now over! If you made something with the prompts, feel free to share with the tags #aughost and/or #aughost2026 (#Aughost 2026 on Tumblr), if you like!

I always enjoy seeing the different ways folks use the prompts. Some folks pick multiple ghosts to split the prompts between and choose which ghost to write for based on the feeling of each prompt word, others interpret the same word multiple ways for different dialogues, and so on. For myself personally, this year I did a mixture of random dialogue, boot and close dialogues, and variant dialogues for existing chains.

I hope everyone got what they wanted out of the event. If you haven't finished all of the prompts and are planning to work on them later, then good luck, you can do it! And I would be very happy to see what you produce with them at any time of year.

Next year I hope to run this again with a fresh set of prompts, and I hope you'll consider joining in!
